Meet Wobble

A little friend for the big stuff.

Growing up brings big feelings. A little friend can help.

Wobble is the first little friend in the Little Classroom Friends world. Through playful characters and gentle stories, the project explores feelings, friendship, curiosity and the little challenges of growing up.

From the classroom

Understanding the feeling before finding the calm.

Wobble & the Big Feeling grew from my experience working with children in the classroom.

At my school, we introduced an anxiety program that helped students understand what happens in their brains and bodies when big feelings take over. We explored the amygdala and “alarm brain”, and practised simple strategies including breathing, mindfulness and sensory awareness.

I saw how helpful these tools could be when children understood why they were feeling the way they were, and had strategies they could return to when things felt overwhelming.

Wobble grew from that experience — a story designed to help children explore big feelings and discover simple ways to find their way back to calm.
