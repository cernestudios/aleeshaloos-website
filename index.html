About me

Hello, I’m Aleesha

A little about me

After many years in the classroom, I’ve seen firsthand the big feelings, curious questions, funny little moments and growing confidence that make childhood so wonderfully messy.

My illustration work draws on that experience, with a focus on playful characters, storytelling, humour and the little details that make a world feel alive.

When I’m not drawing or teaching, you’ll usually find me at the gym, or spending time with my husband and our dog, Rio. I’m a bit of a homebody.
