02 / What shapes a quote

Scope & usage

The creation fee reflects the complexity of the artwork, the number of finished pieces and the time required to develop them properly.

Every quote also defines where, how long and how widely the finished illustration may be used. Additional media, territories, exclusivity, reuse and extended campaigns are priced separately. Copyright remains with Aleesha unless a different written agreement is made.

03 / Working together

Clear from the beginning

  • 50% to beginA booking payment confirms the project and production window.
  • Two sketch roundsTwo consolidated feedback rounds are included unless quoted otherwise.
  • Final paymentThe balance is due before high-resolution final artwork is released.
  • Additional changesChanges outside the agreed brief are quoted separately.
  • Rush workShortened schedules may carry a 25% or greater rush fee.
  • CancellationThe booking payment is non-refundable; completed work is also payable.

A note on licensing

These figures are starting points, not blanket buyout prices. Full copyright transfer is not generally offered. The final proposal will make the creative fee, licensed use, deliverables and timing completely clear before work begins.
