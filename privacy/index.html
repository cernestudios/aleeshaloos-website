Your details, treated with care.

When you request a colouring sheet or join the Wobble waitlist, we use your email address and optional first name to send what you asked for.

You choose the updates

The Wobble waitlist is for Wobble release news. Other artwork, colouring sheets and general news are optional. Downloading a free sheet does not automatically subscribe you to those updates. Marketing emails include an unsubscribe link.

Where your details go

Resend handles email delivery and subscriber preferences. Cloudflare hosts this website and stores signup and consent records used to deliver requests and prepare daily signup summaries for Aleesha. We record your signup source, date and choices. A short-lived, hashed network identifier helps prevent abuse.

We do not sell your details. Download-only signup records are removed after 30 days; consent records are retained while needed to manage your subscription. Email delivery logs follow the provider’s retention settings.

Questions or deletion requests

Email hello@aleeshaloos.com to ask about, correct or delete your information.
